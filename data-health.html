Status definitions and technical detail
  • Retrieval/evidence reports what the retained request and payload establish: Succeeded, No bulletin returned, Unsupported/not applicable only when explicitly asserted, Stale/freshness unknown, Failed transport, or Invalid payload.
  • Planning coverage separately reports Full coverage, Usable partial, Beyond product horizon, Unavailable inside required scope, Elapsed/excluded, or Not applicable.
  • Colour is secondary to the words: stale or unknown freshness and no bulletin are amber; failed transport and invalid payload are red; explicit unsupported/not-applicable, beyond-horizon, and elapsed/excluded outcomes are neutral.
  • Active denominator: full, usable partial, beyond-horizon, and unavailable-inside-required-scope checks are eligible. Elapsed or ended periods and non-applicable products remain visible but do not lower the active coverage ratio.
